# Model implementation review

## Preserved

26 named entities; 42 named relationships; all displayed business keys; relationship attributes for replacement purpose, contribution role, required-metric role, measurement/activity evidence role, and frozen identity verification attributes; source-local customer accounts; separate legal providers/outlets; separate event and periodic evaluation grains; exact rule versions versus stable award identities; explicit benefit units; total/disjoint activity ISA.

## Physical refinements

- Every relation receives an autogenerated integer Id, per manual.
- Entity references in conceptual keys become FKs to unique parent rows, preserving the parent business identity indirectly.
- Eight associative relations add surrogate Id plus UNIQUE pair keys.
- Activity adds SaleId/RentalId/ServiceId, and each subtype retains a unique VehicleActivityId. Bidirectional composite deferred FKs and a CHECK enforce exactly one owned subtype, even for direct SQL writes at commit.
- Award adds AcceptedEvaluationId (unique) and FirstComponentId. A deferred composite FK forces at least one benefit belonging to that award. Additional components may be added; removing the first requires assigning another first within the same transaction.
- Rental replacement is an optional Service FK plus ReplacementPurpose, jointly nullable.
- Intervals are half-open and may be open-ended. Overlapping versions/authorization/enrollment intervals are rejected as an explicit implementation assumption.
- Empty strings are not treated as missing valid identifiers. Non-key email/phone and terminal timestamps may be NULL. Currency is nullable only for noncash benefit components.
- Currency reward values are not aggregated across units. Cash components must use ISO4217 with matching UnitCode/Currency. Points cannot carry a currency.

Business candidate key values are stable after insertion: descriptive fields can be edited, while a new identity requires a new row. This prevents reassigning an outlet or account to another provider and redirecting a beneficiary through a master-data edit.

## Integrity boundaries

Evaluations are final assertions and immutable. Cited activity/subtype and measurement/definition values, RuleVersion and RuleMetric, and measurement contributions become immutable when used by final assessments. Identity evidence rows snapshot actual verified source assertions. Evidence can only be appended before an award accepts the evaluation; accepted evidence is frozen. Live identity links may be revoked later; snapshots are preserved.

This is a bounded relational prototype, not a general evaluator. A new final assessment should be inserted only after a reviewer validates the qualification, authorization and enrollment windows, measurement grain, policy-defined proof path and reward specification. Seed qualification is manually checked synthetic demonstration data. There is no source-revision/rerun identity, payout reversal, redemption, production verification or semantic-web engine.

Changing a legal name does not change registration identity. Business identifiers and source keys are proposed assumptions, not verified real-world identities. Synthetic fixture references must not be submitted as real customer records.
